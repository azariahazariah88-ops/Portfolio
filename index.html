About Me

Who I Am

I'm an ECE graduate from Francis Xavier Engineering College, Tirunelveli, currently working as a Junior Embedded Software Engineer at Cherry Precision Products, Coimbatore. My work sits at the intersection of embedded hardware and software — building systems that bridge the physical and digital worlds.

At Cherry, I develop real-time IoT data acquisition pipelines — reading precision measurements from digital air gauges via Arduino, transmitting data over UART to ESP32 slave devices, connecting to master nodes via Wi-Fi, and visualising everything on live Python dashboards. It's the full stack, from sensor to screen.

Beyond embedded systems, I'm passionate about web development. I build production-ready applications with JavaScript, React.js, and the MERN stack. I also enjoy competitive programming on LeetCode and HackerRank, and I'm always exploring new intersections of hardware and software.
